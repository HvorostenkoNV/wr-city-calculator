# ☭ City Calculator — Workers & Resources: Soviet Republic

**→ [Open the calculator](https://hvorostenkonv.github.io/wr-city-calculator/)**

> Industry-driven development: first you design the industry you want to build,
> then you calculate the city size needed to support it.

## How it works

Enter the **total number of industrial workplaces** — the calculator returns the flats,
population, and every city service needed to support them, with ready-to-build presets.

## What to count as workplaces

**Do NOT count** service jobs — shops, schools, kindergartens, hospitals and other
city services you see in this calculator. Their workers are already included
in the flats coefficient.

**DO count** city infrastructure the calculator doesn't cover: heating plants,
service stations, fire stations, and so on. Use the *actual* staffing you plan,
not the building's maximum — if your fire station will run with 20 firefighters,
count 20, not the full house.

---

*Coefficients are empirical, gathered through gameplay. Found better values? Open an issue.*
